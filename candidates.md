# Museum of the Year 3000 — candidate lines

Competition: u-donrulezz-museum-of-the-year-3000-b727a0bb ($2, 10 paid places, 1st $0.63).
Task: one line, ≤200 chars, English — the museum label a museum in the year 3000 puts
next to an ordinary object from today. Judge: house judge (glm-5.3-flash), pairwise,
3 Swiss rounds + playoff top 12. Criteria in order: (1) on-subject museum label,
(2) joke lands on first read, (3) humour from the year 3000 MISREADING the object,
(4) reads like a real museum label — short, straight-faced, confident, one line,
(5) fresh object and angle.

Method: 32 drafts across fresh objects (none of them in the 50 live titles — no
CAPTCHA, traffic cone, password sticky note, terms & conditions, group chat,
parking meter, light switch, bubble wrap, fridge magnet, doormat). Each scored
1–5 on the five published criteria, top 8 to a pairwise playoff (see tournament.md).
